Immediate Medical Attention Required

If you are experiencing acute testicular pain, severe trauma, or signs of acute infection, do not complete an online form. Proceed to the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.

Warning Signs Requiring Emergency Care

  • !
    Sudden, severe testicular or scrotal pain: Potential testicular torsion. Requires surgical evaluation within 4 to 6 hours to preserve testicular viability.
  • !
    Scrotal trauma: Direct injury followed by rapid swelling, severe bruising, or difficulty urinating.
  • !
    Acute pain with high fever and chills: Severe infection (acute orchitis/epididymitis, urosepsis).
  • !
    Visible blood in urine (gross hematuria) accompanied by acute lower pelvic pain or inability to void.
